# Model card — Credit PD model v2.0

## Model details
- **What it is:** a probability-of-default (PD) model for unsecured consumer loans, plus a decision rule and reason codes.
- **Algorithm:** LightGBM gradient-boosted trees (100 trees, depth 7, class-weighted) inside a scikit-learn pipeline (median/mode imputation, scaling, one-hot encoding), wrapped in Platt (sigmoid) calibration fitted on held-out inner folds. PDs are floored at 0.03% (the Basel minimum) and capped at 99.97%.
- **Decision rule:** decline when calibrated PD ≥ the cut-off stored in `models/pd_model_meta.json`. The cut-off maximises the model's own expected profit and is chosen without using outcomes.
- **Explanations:** the top three factors raising PD for each applicant, from exact TreeSHAP contributions (LightGBM `pred_contrib`) summed back to the applicant-facing field.
- **Code:** `pd_model.py` (model, rule, reasons), `api.py` (serving), `evaluate.py` (evaluation).
- **Owner:** Yash Shelke ([yashshelke2106](https://github.com/yashshelke2106)). Portfolio project, not a production system.

## Intended use
- **In scope:** demonstrating a calibrated, explainable, profit-based credit decision pipeline on public data, and serving as a template for retraining on a lender's own dated data.
- **Out of scope:** real lending decisions. The model has had no out-of-time validation, no reject inference and no regulatory review, and its profit assumptions are illustrative.

## Data
- **Source:** the public Kaggle *Credit Risk Dataset* (`credit_risk_dataset.csv`): 32,581 loans, 12 columns, no dates. Provenance and collection method are undocumented.
- **Preparation:** 7 impossible values (age > 100, employment > 60 years) set to missing; 165 exact duplicate rows removed, leaving 32,416. All imputation happens inside the pipeline.
- **Features used (10):** income, home ownership, employment length, loan purpose, loan grade, loan amount, interest rate, loan-to-income ratio, prior default flag, credit history length.
- **Excluded:** `person_age`, deliberately. It has an IV of 0.0065, and removing it costs about 0.002 ROC-AUC. Excluding it means age can never be cited as a reason for decline.
- **Caution:** `loan_grade` and `loan_int_rate` are outputs of the original lender's underwriting, not facts about the applicant.

## Ethical considerations and fairness
- Approval and default rates are reported by age band in the README. The model is checked for gaps above 10 percentage points between bands with at least 300 applicants.
- Credit history length is a proxy for age, so excluding age does not rule out age-correlated outcomes.
- No other protected attribute (sex, religion, caste, region) is present, so no other group can be checked.
- Reason codes can cite `loan_grade` or `loan_int_rate`. These are lender-assigned, and a regulator would treat them as an inadequate explanation for an adverse action.

## Limitations
See the README's Limitations section. In short: no out-of-time test, reject-inference bias, underwriting outputs used as inputs, assumed LGD and revenue, unlabelled currency, and PSI only a mechanism check.

## Monitoring plan (for a real deployment)
- Compute PSI on the score and CSI on the top five features monthly against the training window. Above 0.10, investigate; above 0.25, retrain.
- Track realised default rate against mean predicted PD by score band each quarter. A persistent gap means the model needs recalibration.
- Track approval rate and approval among good borrowers by age band each quarter, against the 10 pp rule.
